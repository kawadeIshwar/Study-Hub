Place your website screenshots here with the following names:
- home.png (Home page screenshot)
- login.png (Login page screenshot)
- notes.png (Notes page screenshot)
- upload.png (Upload page screenshot)
- communities.png (Communities page screenshot)
- community-detail.png (Community detail with chat)
- profile.png (User profile page)
- teacher-dashboard.png (Teacher dashboard)

You can take screenshots using:
1. Windows: Windows Key + Shift + S
2. Browser DevTools: F12 > Device toolbar > Screenshot
3. Full page screenshot extensions

After adding screenshots, run: node add-screenshots-to-report.js
